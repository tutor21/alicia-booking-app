Guide To Alicia Panoramic Park Tour

Published by TAPP Tours • Adventure Guide

Alicia Panoramic Park, centered around the iconic Binabaje Hills in Alicia, Bohol, is a premier eco-tourism destination offering majestic views of rolling green ridges and sweeping valleys.

What to Expect

The trail takes you across ridge-side pathways surrounded by emerald grass peaks. The hike is manageable for most fitness levels, taking roughly 1 to 2 hours depending on your pace.

Tips For Your Hike

  • Start Early: Begin your trek early in the morning to catch the spectacular sunrise and avoid midday heat.
  • Wear Proper Footwear: Trails can be uneven or slippery after rain; hiking shoes or sneakers with good grip are recommended.
  • Bring Water & Sun Protection: Pack sufficient drinking water, a hat, and sunscreen as parts of the trail have minimal shade.
