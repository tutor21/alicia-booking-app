10 Best Places To Visit In Bohol

Published by TAPP Tours • Bohol Travel Guide

Bohol is one of the most diverse and visually stunning islands in the Philippines. Beyond its world-famous beaches, the province offers breathtaking countryside landscapes, unique geological formations, and rich cultural heritage.

1. Alicia Panoramic Park (Binabaje Hills)

Known for its rolling green hills and stunning sunrise views, this destination has quickly become a premier hiking spot for travelers seeking raw nature away from the crowds.

2. The Chocolate Hills

A globally renowned geological wonder featuring over 1,200 cone-shaped limestone mounds that turn brown during the dry season, resembling chocolate drops.

3. Panglao Island Beaches

Famous for fine white sand beaches, vibrant coral reefs, and incredible scuba diving and snorkeling spots.

4. Loboc River

Take a scenic cruise down the emerald-green Loboc River while enjoying local dishes and traditional music performances.

5. Anda Peninsula

Located on the eastern coast, Anda offers secluded white-sand beaches, crystal-clear freshwater caves (cenotes/pools), and relaxed beach vibes.

6. Tarsier Sanctuary

Home to one of the world's smallest primates, where you can observe these gentle nocturnal creatures in their protected natural habitat.

7. Hinagdanan Cave

A picturesque limestone cave featuring a stunning subterranean lagoon illuminated by natural light filtering through ceiling openings.

8. Baclayon Church

One of the oldest stone churches in the country, showcasing centuries-old architecture and religious artifacts.

9. Danao Adventure Park

An eco-adventure tourism site built for adrenaline seekers, offering ziplining, root climbing, and canyoning over deep gorges.

10. Mahogany Man-Made Forest

A dense two-kilometer stretch of uniform mahogany trees creating a cool, shaded canopy tunnel along the Loboc-Bilar border.
