Best Time To Visit Bohol

Published by TAPP Tours • Travel Guide

Planning a trip to Bohol requires timing your visit around the region's distinct tropical seasons to ensure optimal weather for island hopping, beach lounging, and mountain hiking.

1. Dry Season (November to April) - Recommended

This is widely considered the best time to visit Bohol. The weather is generally sunny, dry, and stable, making it ideal for outdoor excursions, island tours, and hiking trails like Alicia Panoramic Park. December to February brings pleasant, cooler breezes, while March and April offer warm tropical weather perfect for beaches.

2. Wet / Rainy Season (July to October)

During these months, tropical rain showers and occasional typhoons can occur. While tourist crowds are significantly smaller and prices can be lower, outdoor hiking and boat tours may experience cancellations due to weather conditions.

3. Shoulder Season (May and June)

The transition period between dry and wet seasons brings warm temperatures and intermittent showers. It's a great time if you want fewer tourists while still enjoying clear weather windows.
